Local Regulations and Permits

One of the first things to check is whether you need a permit to place a dumpster on the street or in a public area. In Arlington, the Department of Public Works typically requires a permit for dumpsters that obstruct public ways. This ensures that the safety and accessibility of streets and sidewalks are maintained.
