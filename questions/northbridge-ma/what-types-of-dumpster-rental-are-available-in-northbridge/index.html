Overview of Dumpster Sizes

Here’s a quick breakdown of each dumpster size and the types of projects they are best suited for:

  • 10-Yard Dumpster: Ideal for small cleanout projects such as garage cleanouts, minor renovations, or landscaping debris. This size holds approximately 2-3 pickup truck loads of waste.
  • 15-Yard Dumpster: Perfect for medium-sized projects, including bathroom remodels or small kitchen renovations. It can accommodate about 4-5 pickup truck loads.
  • 20-Yard Dumpster: A great choice for larger renovations, such as a full kitchen remodel or significant yard cleanup. This dumpster holds roughly 6-8 pickup truck loads of waste.
  • 30-Yard Dumpster: Suitable for large construction jobs or significant cleanouts, like estate cleanouts or major home renovations, with a capacity of around 10-12 pickup truck loads.
  • 40-Yard Dumpster: The largest option, ideal for large-scale construction or demolition projects. This size can contain about 14-16 pickup truck loads of waste.
