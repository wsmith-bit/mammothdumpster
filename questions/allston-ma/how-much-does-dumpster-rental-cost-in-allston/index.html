Understanding Dumpster Rental Costs in Allston, MA

When considering a dumpster rental in Allston, MA, you'll find that pricing can vary based on several factors, including the size of the dumpster, the rental duration, and any additional services you may require. On average, residents and contractors can expect to pay between $300 to $600 for a standard rental period of seven days. This cost typically encompasses the base fee for the dumpster, delivery, pickup, and disposal of waste, which is often calculated based on weight.

Additional Costs and Considerations

While the base rental price covers a lot, there are additional factors to consider:

  • Extended Rental Periods: If your project takes longer than the standard 7-day rental, you can extend the rental for a daily fee, typically around $10 to $15 per day.
  • Overweight Fees: If your dumpster exceeds the weight limit agreed upon, additional charges may apply based on the tonnage over the limit.
  • Permit Fees: If you're placing a dumpster on a public street or in a location requiring a permit, additional fees may be charged. It's essential to check with local regulations in Allston regarding this requirement.
