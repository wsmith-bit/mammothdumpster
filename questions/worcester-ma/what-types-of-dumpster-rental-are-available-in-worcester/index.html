How to Choose the Right Dumpster for Your Project

Choosing the right dumpster size is crucial for efficient waste management. Here are some tips to guide you:

  • Assess the Volume of Waste: Consider the amount of waste your project will generate. For a simple decluttering job, a 10 or 15-yard dumpster might suffice, while larger renovations may require a 20-yard or bigger dumpster.
  • Type of Materials: Identify the materials you’ll be disposing of. If your project includes heavy materials like concrete or dirt, opting for a larger dumpster ensures you won’t exceed weight limits.
  • Project Duration: Think about how long you’ll need the dumpster. Most rentals come with a standard 7-day period, but you can easily extend this based on your project timeline.
  • Neighborhood Considerations: If you’re in a densely populated area like the Canal District, where street space is limited, choosing a smaller dumpster might be more practical and easier to manage.
