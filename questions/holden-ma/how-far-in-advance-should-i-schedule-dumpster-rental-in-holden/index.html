Understanding the Busy Seasons

Holden's climate and local culture play a significant role in the timing of projects that necessitate dumpster rentals. In these busy seasons, it's not uncommon for rental companies to experience higher demand, which can lead to limited availability.

Typical Booking Windows

For most residential projects, a lead time of 1 to 2 weeks is ideal. This allows ample time for the scheduling of delivery and pickup and ensures that you can secure the dumpster size you require. If you're managing a construction site or a larger project, you may want to consider booking even further in advance, potentially 2 to 4 weeks, particularly if you're working with multiple contractors or have a specific project timeline to meet.
