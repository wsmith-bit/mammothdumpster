Understanding Dumpster Rental Costs in North Weymouth

When considering dumpster rental in North Weymouth, Massachusetts, the costs can vary based on several factors, including the size of the dumpster, the duration of the rental, and the type of waste you need to dispose of. Generally, homeowners and contractors can expect to pay between $300 to $600 for a standard rental. This price typically includes delivery, pickup, and a 7-day rental period. However, additional costs may apply depending on specific needs and project requirements.

Base Rates and Size Options

Mammoth Dumpster offers five different dumpster sizes that cater to a variety of projects, from small residential cleanouts to larger construction jobs. Here’s a breakdown of the typical costs associated with each size:

  • 10-yard dumpster: Approximately $300 – Ideal for small cleanup projects or minor renovations.
  • 15-yard dumpster: Around $350 – Suitable for medium-sized renovations or garage cleanouts.
  • 20-yard dumpster: About $450 – Commonly used for larger home renovations or commercial projects.

What’s Included in the Rental

The base rate usually covers:

  • Delivery and pickup of the dumpster.
  • A 7-day rental period.
  • Waste disposal costs based on weight (tonnage).

Additionally, every rental includes driveway protection mats to ensure your property surfaces are safeguarded during the rental period. This is particularly important in neighborhoods like East Weymouth, where driveway space can be limited and maintaining property aesthetics is crucial.

Potential Add-Ons and Extra Costs

While the base rental fee covers several services, there can be additional charges that you should be aware of:

  • Extended Rental Periods: If you require the dumpster for more than 7 days, there will be a daily fee, typically around $10 to $15 per day.
  • Overweight Charges: If your waste exceeds the weight limit included in your rental, additional fees will apply based on the tonnage over the limit.
  • Special Waste Disposal: Certain materials may incur extra charges if they require special handling or disposal.
