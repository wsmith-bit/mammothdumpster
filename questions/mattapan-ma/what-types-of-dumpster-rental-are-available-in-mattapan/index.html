Choosing the Right Size for Your Project

When selecting a dumpster size, consider the nature and volume of the waste. For instance, if you live in the Harambee Park area and are planning an extensive home renovation, a 20-yard or 30-yard dumpster may be the best choice to accommodate the excess materials. In contrast, if you are simply cleaning out your garage, a 10-yard dumpster might suffice.

Additionally, think about the weight of the materials you’re disposing of. Each dumpster size has a weight limit, and exceeding this can lead to additional costs. For instance, heavier materials like concrete and bricks will fill up a dumpster faster than lighter materials such as cardboard or wood. In Mattapan, where many homes are older and may require significant renovations, having the right size can save time and prevent unexpected fees.
