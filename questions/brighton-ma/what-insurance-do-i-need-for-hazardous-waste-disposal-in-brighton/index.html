Understanding Insurance for Hazardous Waste Disposal in Brighton

If you're a homeowner in Brighton, Massachusetts, planning a renovation or decluttering project, you might be wondering, what insurance do I need for hazardous waste disposal? This concern is especially relevant for homes built before 1980, as they may contain hazardous materials like lead paint or asbestos, which require special handling and disposal.

What Insurance Typically Covers

When it comes to hazardous waste disposal, homeowners should know that general liability insurance is essential. This insurance often covers accidents that may occur during the project, such as damage to your property or injuries to third parties while the dumpster is on your property. However, keep in mind that standard homeowner's insurance typically does not cover hazardous waste disposal unless specifically mentioned in the policy. Therefore, you should inquire about coverage for hazardous materials when discussing your policy with your insurance agent.
