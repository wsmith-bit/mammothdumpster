Common Extra Fees to Consider

  • Overage Charges: One of the most common fees is associated with exceeding the weight limit of your rented dumpster. Each dumpster size has a specified weight limit, typically ranging from 2 to 3 tons. If your waste exceeds this limit, expect to pay an additional charge per ton over the limit. For instance, if you're clearing out a home in North Grafton and have more heavy debris than anticipated, you could face additional costs.
  • Delivery and Pickup Fees: While many companies include standard delivery and pickup in their rental price, additional charges may apply for special circumstances. For example, if you need your dumpster placed in a difficult-to-access location or if you require same-day service, extra fees could be incurred.
  • Permit Costs: Depending on where you plan to place the dumpster, you may need a permit, especially if it's on a public street or sidewalk in Grafton. Check with the local Grafton authorities to find out the specific requirements and associated costs for obtaining a permit.
