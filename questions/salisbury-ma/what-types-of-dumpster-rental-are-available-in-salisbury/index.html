Available Dumpster Sizes

Mammoth Dumpster provides five distinct sizes of roll-off dumpsters:

  • 10-yard dumpster: Ideal for small projects such as home decluttering or minor renovations. This size can hold about 2 tons of waste, accommodating items like old furniture or small amounts of yard waste.
  • 15-yard dumpster: Suitable for medium-sized projects, including bathroom remodels or garage cleanouts. It can handle approximately 3 tons of material, making it a versatile choice for residential cleanouts.
  • 20-yard dumpster: Perfect for larger renovations or construction projects. This size can accommodate around 4 tons of debris, including drywall, wood, and other construction materials.
  • 30-yard dumpster: This option is designed for significant construction or remodeling jobs, allowing for up to 6 tons of waste. It’s commonly used by contractors managing multiple projects in areas like Salisbury’s commercial districts.
  • 40-yard dumpster: The largest option, ideal for extensive renovations, commercial cleanouts, or large construction jobs. This dumpster size can hold around 8 tons of waste, making it a popular choice for large-scale operations.
