Additional Costs and Considerations

While the base rental rates are competitive, there are several factors that can affect your final bill:

  • Weight Overages: Most rentals include a specific weight limit (usually around 1 ton). If your waste exceeds this limit, additional fees will apply, typically around $75 per additional ton.
  • Extended Rental Periods: If you need the dumpster longer than the standard 7 days, you can extend your rental for a daily fee, generally ranging from $10 to $15 per day.
  • Specialty Materials: Certain types of waste, such as concrete or dirt, may incur additional disposal fees, so it’s important to clarify what materials are accepted upfront.
