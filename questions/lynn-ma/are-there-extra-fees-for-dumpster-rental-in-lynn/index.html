Understanding Extra Fees for Dumpster Rental in Lynn

Common Extra Fees to Consider

  • Overage Charges: One of the most common extra fees is the overage charge for exceeding the weight limit of the dumpster. For instance, if you rent a 20-yard dumpster, it typically has a weight limit of around 3 tons. If your waste exceeds this limit, you may incur additional charges based on the tonnage over the limit.
  • Delivery Fees: While many companies include delivery in their base price, certain conditions may lead to extra delivery fees. For example, if your property is located in a hard-to-access area or requires special equipment for delivery, additional charges may apply.
  • Permit Costs: In some cases, especially if you are placing the dumpster on a public road or sidewalk, you may need a permit from the city of Lynn. The cost of this permit can vary, so it’s essential to check with local regulations before placing your order.
  • Extended Rental Fees: If you find that you need the dumpster for longer than the standard rental period (typically 7 days), you may incur additional daily rental fees. This is particularly relevant if you are undertaking a larger project in neighborhoods like Lynnfield or along the waterfront that may take more time to complete.
