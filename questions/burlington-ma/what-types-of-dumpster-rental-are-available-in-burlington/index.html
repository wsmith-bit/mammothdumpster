Choosing the Right Dumpster for Your Project

Choosing the right dumpster size is essential for efficient waste management. Here are some guidelines to help you decide:

  • If you're planning a small renovation or decluttering session, start with a 10 or 15-yard dumpster. These are often sufficient for disposing of household junk, furniture, or minor construction debris.
  • For medium to large projects, such as kitchen remodels or extensive yard cleanups, consider a 20 or 30-yard dumpster. These sizes can handle the additional volume of materials without overflowing.
  • For significant commercial renovations or large-scale construction jobs, opt for the 40-yard dumpster, which can accommodate large amounts of debris and waste.
